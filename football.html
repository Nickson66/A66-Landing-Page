Three outcomes: home win, draw, away win. Also called 1X2. Odds reflect the probability of each outcome.

Asian handicap eliminates the draw by giving one side a goal advantage or deficit. -0.5 means the favourite must win. +0.5 means the underdog wins or draws.

Live markets update during the match. Odds change with score and time. A stable connection is recommended. The betting window is shorter than pre-match.

FAQ

What is Asian handicap? +
A market that removes the draw by applying a goal advantage or deficit to one team.
Can I bet live? +
Yes. Live betting is available on most matches. Odds update in real time.
