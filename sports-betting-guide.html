No dedicated application is required.

Understanding Odds Formats

A66 displays odds primarily in decimal format. Decimal odds represent the total return per unit staked, including the original stake. A price of 2.50 means that a successful bet of 100 returns 250 in total (150 profit plus the 100 stake). A price of 1.40 returns 140 on a 100 stake.

Lower decimal figures indicate outcomes the book considers more probable. A price near 1.20 reflects a strong favourite. A price near 2.00 reflects a roughly even assessment. Higher figures indicate less probable outcomes. Implied probability can be estimated by dividing 1 by the decimal odds. A price of 2.00 implies approximately 50 percent. Bookmaker margin means the combined implied probabilities of all outcomes in a market normally exceed 100 percent.

Core Markets

Match Result (1X2)

The match-result market offers three outcomes: home win, draw, or away win. It is the most direct market. Settlement is based on the final result after any designated extra time or according to the specific rules of the competition. No handicap is applied.

Asian Handicap

Asian Handicap removes the draw by applying a goal or point advantage or deficit to one side. A -1.5 handicap on the favourite requires that side to win by two or more for the bet to succeed. A +1.5 on the underdog succeeds if that side loses by one, draws, or wins. Whole-number handicaps can result in a push (stake refunded) if the margin matches the line exactly. Quarter-goal lines split the stake across two adjacent handicaps.

Over / Under

Over/Under markets set a total for goals, points, or other statistics. Over 2.5 requires three or more goals. Under 2.5 requires two or fewer. The threshold varies by event. Settlement follows the published total for that market.

Live Betting

Live betting markets are available on selected events while play is in progress. Odds update according to the current state of the event. The time available to place a live bet is limited. A stable connection is recommended because prices and market availability can change quickly. Once a live bet is accepted it is subject to the same settlement rules as pre-match bets on that market.

Access and Account

The sportsbook is accessed through a web browser on desktop or mobile. A66 does not provide a separate sports betting application. The balance used for sports wagers is the same balance used for casino games. Deposits and withdrawals follow the platform’s standard procedures. Market rules and maximum stakes are displayed before bet confirmation. Players should verify the selection, odds, and stake before submitting.
