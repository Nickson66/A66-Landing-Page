How Baccarat Works

Baccarat involves two hands: banker and player. Cards are dealt according to fixed rules. The hand closest to nine wins. Players bet on banker, player, or tie before the deal.

Card values: Ace = 1, 2-9 = face value, 10 and face cards = 0. Hand totals over 9 drop the tens digit. A third card may be drawn based on fixed rules.

Banker: Lower house edge, usually with 5% commission on wins.
Player: Slightly higher house edge, no commission.
Tie: Higher payout, higher house edge.

Tables vary in limits, commission rates, and side bets. Review table info before joining. Lower limits suit smaller budgets. Higher limits are available for larger stakes.
