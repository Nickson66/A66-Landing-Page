Withdrawal processing times depend on the method selected and on the completion of verification. Under normal conditions, approved withdrawals are completed within a short period. Delays can occur when additional review is required or when the selected method experiences processing constraints. Players can check the status of a pending withdrawal in the account transaction history.

Support and Account Management

Account settings allow players to update contact details, review transaction history, and manage security options. Game Providers and Fairness

Slot and live casino content is supplied by independent providers. These providers operate their own random number generators and studio environments. A66 does not alter game outcomes or adjust return percentages. Players who wish to verify the characteristics of a title should consult the information published by the provider within the game client.

Live dealer games are streamed from studios operated by the providers. The physical dealing, spinning, or rolling is observable in the video feed. Results correspond to the physical outcome. Promotions and Wagering

Promotional offers are listed in the promotions area. Each offer includes terms that define eligibility, the deposit required, the wagering multiple, game contribution rates, and the expiry period. Bonus funds and winnings derived from bonus play are subject to the stated requirements. Withdrawal of these amounts is available only after the requirements are met.
