Responsible Gaming

A66 provides tools and information to help players manage their gambling activity. Gambling should be treated as entertainment with a cost, not as a source of income.

Deposit Limits

Players can set daily, weekly, or monthly deposit limits in their account settings. A limit restricts the total amount that can be deposited during the selected period. Decreases to a limit take effect immediately. Increases take effect after the current period ends. This delay is intentional and cannot be bypassed.

Session Reminders

Optional session reminders alert players to the length of time spent on the platform. Reminders can be set at intervals selected by the player. They do not interrupt play but provide a prompt to consider whether to continue. Session reminders are configured in account settings.

Self-Exclusion

Self-exclusion allows a player to block access to their account for a defined period. During exclusion, login is prevented and promotional communications are suspended. Requests are processed by support. Self-exclusion cannot be reversed before the stated end date. Players who wish to exclude should contact support with their preferred duration.

Warning Signs

The following may indicate that gambling is becoming difficult to control:

  • Spending more than planned or using money intended for essential expenses
  • Attempting to recover losses by increasing stakes
  • Hiding gambling activity from family or friends
  • Feeling unable to stop or reduce play

Players who recognise these patterns are encouraged to use the available tools or contact support for information on external assistance.

Contact

For questions about responsible gaming tools or to request self-exclusion, contact support through live chat. Conversations are handled confidentially.
