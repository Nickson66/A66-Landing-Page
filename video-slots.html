What Are Video Slots?

Video slots are the dominant format in modern online gaming. Unlike classic three-reel machines, video slots typically feature five reels, multiple paylines or ways-to-win systems, and interactive bonus features. The A66 library includes titles from various providers, each with distinct visual styles and mathematical models.

Bonus Features

Free spins are the most common feature, triggered by scatter symbols. During free spins, multipliers, extra wilds, or modified reels may apply. Some titles offer a choice of modes with different risk profiles.

Other mechanics include hold-and-spin, where symbols lock while others respin; cascading reels, where wins are removed and new symbols fall; and expanding wilds that cover entire reels.

Understanding Volatility

High volatility means less frequent but potentially larger wins. Low volatility means more frequent smaller returns. Choose based on your budget and preference. Neither is better—they suit different playing styles.

RTP (return to player) is a long-term theoretical average. It does not predict short-term results. A 96% RTP game can still produce losing sessions.
