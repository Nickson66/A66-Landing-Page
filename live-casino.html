Baccarat

Baccarat compares two hands called Player and Banker. Each hand is scored using the final digit of its card total. Additional cards are drawn according to fixed table rules, rather than decisions made by the player.

Player, Banker and Tie marketsFixed card-drawing rulesCommission varies by table
Hand values

Aces count as one; tens and face cards count as zero. Other cards keep their face value, and only the final digit of the total is used.

Fixed drawing rules

Whether an additional card is dealt depends on the published rules. The player does not choose to hit or stand.

Commission and variants

Banker wins may carry a commission. No-commission tables can use different payout conditions; check the table’s rules.

Table differences
